Myxa.js

A house fly on your page. And something on the edges, watching it. Source and docs on GitHub

The fly walks, stops to rub its front feet together, cleans its wings with a hind leg, takes off, darts across the page and lands somewhere else. Bring the pointer near it and it hurries off; move at it quickly and it takes off before you get close.

It is drawn on a single transparent canvas over the whole page, and the canvas is completely intangible: every click, scroll, selection and drag goes through to the page underneath, and it never takes focus. Try it: the field and button below work whether or not the fly is sitting on them.

A jumping spider lives on the borders of the page. It does not build webs; it stalks. It sits and watches, creeps along the edge towards the fly, freezes when the fly moves, and leaps from about seven body lengths out. The fly ignores it creeping up but bolts at the leap, and usually gets away.

Under the lens

A small arena of its own, with its own fly and spider. The camera rides on one of them, so the animal stays in the middle of the box and the ground moves under it. Slow time down to see the legs at work.
